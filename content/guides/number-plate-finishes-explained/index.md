The finish is the personality of a number plate. Here is what each option actually is, how it is made, and the look it gives your car.

## Glossy

A glossy plate is clearcoated to a mirror-smooth, wet-look shine. It suits cars with paintwork that already carries a deep gloss.

- [Glossy Black Euro](/catalogue/glossy-black-euro/) - the classic black European-style plate with a high-gloss finish
- [Glossy White](/catalogue/glossy-white/) - bright white baseplate for the traditional Singapore front-plate look

## Matte & satin

Matte finishes remove glare entirely and photograph beautifully, which is why they are popular with owners who park their cars under harsh lights.

- [Basic Satin Matte](/catalogue/basic-satin-matte/) - soft, low-sheen everyday finish
- [3D Matte-on-Matte](/catalogue/3d-matte-on-matte/) - matte characters on a matte baseplate for a subtle, monochrome look
- [Rough Matte](/catalogue/rough-matte/) - textured, non-glare surface with reflective laminated characters

## Pearl

Pearl finishes are spray-painted with MIPA automotive pearl coating before clearcoat, giving the plate a shimmer that shifts with the light - closest thing to matching modern car paint.

- [Pearl White](/catalogue/pearl-white/) and [Pearl Black](/catalogue/pearl-black/)

## Metallic

Metallic silver is also spray-painted, and comes in two flake sizes: small flake for a fine, OEM-like sparkle, or big flake for a bold, show-car glint.

- [Metallic Silver](/catalogue/metallic-silver/)

## Carbon weave

A carbon-weave finish mimics real carbon fibre - available in metallic or matte carbon. It is printed, not hydro-dipped or stickered, so the weave pattern stays crisp under clearcoat.

- [Carbon Weave](/catalogue/carbon-weave/)

## Specialty & retro

Beyond the mainstream, there are statement options: [Frosted](/catalogue/frosted/) translucent plates, [Rainbow Stars](/catalogue/rainbow-stars/) - characters laminated in Oracal road-safety reflective film that sparkles at night - and the [Classic Retro](/catalogue/classic-retro/) metallic-silver vintage font.

## A practical note on care

All our plates carry UV- and chemical-resistant coatings, but a 30-second rinse when you wash the car keeps the characters bright for years. Avoid abrasive polishers directly on printed characters.
