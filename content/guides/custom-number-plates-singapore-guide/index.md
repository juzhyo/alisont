Ordering custom plates for your car, motorcycle or e-bike in Singapore is a five-minute job if you know what you want. Here is the full process.

## Step 1: Pick your finish

Start with the [finishes guide](/guides/number-plate-finishes-explained/) or browse the [catalogue](/catalogue/) to choose glossy, matte, pearl, metallic, carbon or a specialty look. Every style is available as a custom 3D-printed plate.

## Step 2: Decide standard or fully custom

- **Standard sizing** keeps your plate within strict OneMotoring dimensional standards (70mm H x 50mm W x 10mm), precision-engineered for daily road use.
- **Full custom dimensions or track-spec styling** are available for off-road, show and display purposes - just tell us what you are building.

Motorcycles get compact plates engineered for bike requirements; e-bikes use the compliant 35mm x 25mm sizing. See [motorcycle plates](/catalogue/motorcycle-plates/) and [e-bike plates](/catalogue/e-bike-plates/).

## Step 3: Fine-tune the details

- **Kerning**: our [kerning-adjusted](/catalogue/kerning-adjusted/) option balances the spacing between characters, eliminating awkward gaps for a premium finish.
- **Logos**: add your own design ([custom logo](/catalogue/custom-logo/)) or a raised 3D emblem ([custom 3D logo](/catalogue/custom-3d-logo/)).
- **Extras**: diecast collectors can pair a plate with a custom [acrylic display case](/catalogue/custom-diecast-acrylic-case/).

## Step 4: Order and wait 24 hours

Once your combination is confirmed, most orders are produced within 24 hours (1 business day). We will confirm the exact ETA before we start, and delivery is arranged the moment your plates are ready.

## Step 5: Mount and enjoy

Plates use strong adhesive bonding with no gap between the characters and baseplate - and because every plate is VEP/ANPR friendly, gantry and customs cameras read it without drama.
