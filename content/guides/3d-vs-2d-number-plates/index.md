If you are shopping for custom car plates in Singapore, the first fork in the road is 3D versus 2D. Here is the honest difference, without the marketing spin.

## What makes a plate "3D"

A 3D number plate uses solid, raised characters that stand off the baseplate - think of classic embossed plates, but sharper. At AlisonT Design, the characters are 3D-printed as solid blocks, then bonded to a laser-cut cast acrylic baseplate with no gap between letter and plate.

Because the lettering has physical depth, it catches light differently from every angle. That depth is the main reason owners choose 3D plates: they read as a deliberate styling upgrade rather than a factory part.

## How 2D plates compare

Flat (2D) plates print or cut the characters level with the surface. They are lighter, cheaper to produce, and easier to make compliant, but they offer none of the dimensional character that makes a plate look custom from ten metres away.

## Will 3D plates pass ANPR and gantry readers?

Our 3D plates are engineered with high-contrast characters that remain clearly visible at all distances and angles, and they are VEP/ANPR friendly - readable by the camera systems used at customs and condominium gantries. If you drive in and out of Malaysia regularly, this matters.

## Durability in Singapore weather

A plate sits in the sun, rain and heat every single day. Our 3D characters are finished with automotive-grade coatings - including German MIPA 2K-HS clearcoat on premium lines - over UV- and chemical-resistant acrylic, so the raised lettering keeps its sharp edge instead of yellowing or chipping.
